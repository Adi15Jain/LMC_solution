# Earth asset credits

All imagery below is from NASA and is in the public domain under NASA's
media usage guidelines. Attribution is not legally required; it is given
because the sources are worth knowing and the assets are reproducible.

Regenerate with `python scripts/prepare_earth.py`.

## `earth_day.webp`
Blue Marble Next Generation, December 2004 - topography and bathymetry.
<https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73909/world.topo.bathy.200412.3x5400x2700.jpg>

## `earth_night.webp`
Earth's city lights (DMSP).
<https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/55000/55167/earth_lights_lrg.jpg>

## `earth_normal.webp`
GEBCO elevation, converted to a tangent-space normal map.
<https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/73000/73934/gebco_08_rev_elev_21600x10800.png>

## `earth_clouds.webp`
MODIS cloud composite.
<https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_2048.jpg>
